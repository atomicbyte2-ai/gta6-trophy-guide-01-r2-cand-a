The road to Platinum

Three phases, about 200 hours. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals — long, not hard (4/10). Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off.

1

Story

60–80 hours · Chapters 1–5

  • Play all 5 chapters; do optional missions as they appear.
  • Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals as you go.
  • Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation — then keep a manual save at each chapter start.
  • Story trophies pop as you play; some land at the start of the next chapter.
2

100% checklist

~100 hours · after the story

  • Open the checklist under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — earlier work counts.
  • Complete 70 of 80 Challenges; 300 stunts; all collectibles.
  • Rob all 39 illegal operations; discover 200 of 277 vehicles.
  • Finish the stranger strands and the drone, race and shooting-range sets.
3

Clean-up

~10 hours · free roam

  • Strangers & Freaks strands and anything the checklist still shows.
  • Bank vaults, jewelry jobs and the last collectible card sets.
  • Check the missables tab before you call it done.
  • Your 54th trophy triggers the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

⚠ The one possibly missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation (the morning after Down to Earth, once Lucia is home — follow her to Jason's house balcony and accept) disappears after Chapter 1. Do it anyway, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. It is not 100% confirmed that the balcony spot counts — but missing it is the one mistake you cannot undo.

Missables & disputes

Only one trophy can be missed, and only one has sources that genuinely differ. Everything else is unmissable or stays open after the story.
